When did dinosaurs live?

Dinosaurs lived during the Mesozoic Era, which lasted from about 252 to 66 million years ago.

Triassic

252–201 million years ago. The earliest dinosaurs appeared, and many were relatively small.

Jurassic

201–145 million years ago. Dinosaurs became widespread, including enormous long-necked sauropods.

Cretaceous

145–66 million years ago. Famous dinosaurs such as Tyrannosaurus and Triceratops lived during this period.

Birds are living dinosaurs!

Modern birds evolved from small, feathered theropod dinosaurs. So when you see a bird, you're seeing a living branch of the dinosaur family tree.
